Time is ticking, make it count!
In your time traveling adventures, you've somehow ended up stranded in the treacherous year of 2026! Your time machine can only go backwards, and only for a second at a time! Harness the Sands of Time to spin yourself right back to the epoch's onset and make your way home, hopefully...
